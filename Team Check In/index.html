Simple By Design.

Team Check-In is built around one idea: everyone should know what needs to happen next.

STEP 01

CHECK IN

Team members enter the workflow and see what needs attention.

STEP 02

CLAIM IT

Someone takes responsibility for the task or assignment.

STEP 03

DO THE WORK

The team completes the assignment and provides whatever proof is required.

STEP 04

CHECK IT OFF

Everyone sees the updated status and the workflow keeps moving.
